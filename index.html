Budget Tracker

Budget Tracker is a personal, single-user budgeting tool. It reads card purchase and deposit alert emails from its owner's own Gmail account, records each transaction in a database stored on the owner's computer, and shows spending against monthly budgets.

It is not a commercial product and is not offered to the public.

How it uses Gmail

  • Reads only bank and card alert emails to extract the date, merchant, and amount.
  • Adds a "Budget/Processed" label to emails it has recorded.
  • Moves processed alert emails older than 90 days to Trash.
